# Framing Motion Template

**Framing Motion Template** is a cinematic Framer portfolio for filmmakers, video editors, motion designers and creative studios.

## Overview

The editable template combines bold typography and cinematic imagery with a CMS-powered work portfolio, motion presentation and responsive layouts.

## Template Features

- Cinematic homepage and featured projects
- Work portfolio with six demonstration projects and individual project pages
- Services, About and Contact pages
- Contact enquiry form that requires a buyer-configured destination
- Video and motion presentation
- Desktop, tablet and mobile layouts

## Technical Approach and CMS Architecture

The project uses Framer layouts, reusable page structures and a Projects CMS collection. The Work portfolio and individual project pages draw on project content such as titles, imagery, media settings and case-study details. The homepage's three featured selections are edited separately from the Projects CMS.

## Responsive Design and Media

The template provides desktop, tablet and phone layouts. Project media supports posters and optional video, with motion used in the presentation. Buyers should check their own media, links and layouts before publishing.

## Customization

Customers receive access through a Framer remix link and can customize the copied project in Framer. Replace the fictional demonstration work, branding and content, and configure a destination for the contact form.

## Validation

Documented historical checks include a native Framer CMS editing and canvas-binding test, owner review of desktop, tablet and phone presentation, and extraction and integrity checks of the local customer access ZIP. These checks do not establish live checkout, delivery, form receipt or every browser interaction.
